Expert Guidance

NUTRITION ADVICE

Your Goals, Our Expertise
FUELLING YOUR
PERFORMANCE

Whether you're chasing a PB, cutting for a comp, or just getting started — our in-house nutritionists have built proven protocols for every goal.

BUILDING MUSCLE

To build lean muscle, aim for 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily. Prioritise post-workout protein within 30 minutes of training — whey concentrate or isolate works best for absorption speed.

FAT LOSS

Maintain a moderate calorie deficit while keeping protein high to preserve muscle. Carb timing matters — front-load carbs around your training window and reduce them in the evening for best results.

ENDURANCE & PERFORMANCE

Endurance athletes need reliable energy — prioritise complex carbohydrates like Cream of Rice before long sessions. Electrolytes during training and a fast-digesting protein post-session accelerates recovery.

RECOVERY & SLEEP

Recovery is where gains are made. A casein protein shake before bed provides a slow-release amino acid supply overnight. Magnesium supplementation has also been shown to improve sleep quality and reduce muscle cramps.

PLANT-BASED ATHLETES

A well-planned plant-based diet can fully support athletic performance. Blend pea and rice protein to achieve a complete amino acid profile. Pay particular attention to B12, iron, and omega-3 intake.

GET A FREE CONSULTATION

Not sure where to start? Our in-house nutritionists offer free 1-to-1 consultations to build a supplement stack personalised to your goals, budget, and lifestyle. No generic advice — just what works for you.
